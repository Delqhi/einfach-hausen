# External launch blockers — legal, privacy and public trust

**Status:** 2026-08-23
**Scope:** public legal, privacy, contact and trust statements for Einfach Hausen.

This register is deliberately narrower than the engineering backlog. It contains only facts or approvals that require an external business, legal, tax, privacy or documentary authority before they may be published. Missing product code is not converted into an “external blocker”.

## Rules for this register

- One missing authority fact appears in exactly one row. Other pages reference that row conceptually instead of duplicating it.
- No placeholder value may be promoted to a public fact merely to make a page look complete.
- “Not applicable” is also an authority decision where applicability depends on the actual operator or business model.
- A public guarantee, certification, seal or award is optional. If it is omitted, the missing claim does not block a launch; if it is advertised, documentary/legal approval is mandatory first.
- The current pages are production-structured but explicitly **not represented as legally reviewed or legally complete** until the applicable rows below are closed.

## Legal-source checkpoints used for page structure

These sources define the **structure to verify**, not a substitute for legal review of the actual operator:

- DDG § 5: provider identification such as name/address, electronic contact and operator-specific register/supervisory/tax disclosures where applicable.
- GDPR Articles 13/14: controller/contact, purposes/legal bases, recipients, retention, rights and transfer/automated-processing information where applicable.
- TDDDG § 25: consent analysis for storing/accessing information on an end user’s device, with exceptions for strictly necessary functionality.
- VSBG § 36: website/AGB information about participation in consumer dispute resolution, subject to applicability and statutory exceptions.

## Close-out rule

T-0031 may be completed as an engineering/content-structure task when the pages, lint/build and link smoke are green and this register is current. **A production legal-readiness claim may not be made** until all launch-blocking rows above are resolved by their named owners. Optional trust claims (`EH-T01`, `EH-T02`) may remain unresolved only while those claims remain absent from public copy.
