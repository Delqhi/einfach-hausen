## Direction and design rationale
Working name: Ein Zuhause mit Gedächtnis. Brand claim remains "Dein Haus. Einfach geregelt."
Create three repeatable signatures:
1. Hauskante: one restrained clipped corner (45 degrees at a shoulder/corner), used on a large image and on the house record cover. It is a framing grammar derived from architecture, not a replacement trademark.
2. Hauslinie: crisp rules, register numbers and connected dated entries show continuity. Use lines with real grouping/navigation purpose; no decorative accent borders or arbitrary dashboard tiles.
3. Word scale: bold Inter headlines at meaningful editorial scale, short measures, generous but intentional spacing; contrast with precise small metadata. Preserve the logo's own handwritten part rather than adding an unrelated script or serif.

Palette: existing petrol #105258, deep #0a3539, paper #faf8f4, ink #10222a, muted #4b5b60, line #e4e2dc, sand #ecdfc9 and terracotta #a84d29. A narrow decorative ochre/terracotta marker must never masquerade as service status.
Composition: large asymmetric opening with photograph and integrated house-record insert; linear three-promise section; deep petrol house-memory chapter with purposeful line-art house and selected physical system -> corresponding record; light practical homeowner interface and personal contact.
Typography: existing self-hosted InterVariable, weights 400–750, responsive display 54–112px, section 40–64px, body 16–18px, metadata 12–13px. No new font requests.
Photography: existing original project assets only. No fake testimonials, no invented ratings, no invented company details. Photos illustrate the proposed mood, not verified clients or staff.
Motion: brief entrance/state transition only, no infinite or scroll-hijacking effect; reduce-motion path has no animation.
